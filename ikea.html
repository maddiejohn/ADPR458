"Home Moves With You" Campaign

A campaign exploring how IKEA helps young adults create a sense of home, even in temporary or shared spaces.

Creative Brief

IKEA’s goal was to connect with Gen Z and young Millennials living in dorms, apartments, or shared spaces who want their environments to feel personal, even if they are temporary or on a budget.

The campaign communicates that IKEA makes it possible to create a space that feels like home—no matter where you are.
