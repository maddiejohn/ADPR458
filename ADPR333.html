"That's Walmart?" Campaign

Young Ones Case Study Video

Created in collaboration with Livia Ziskey, Nina Kudlacz, and Payton Pohlad.

Problem

Gen Z does not think of Walmart when they think about fashion.

Insight

College students love being surprised by style and value.

Solution

Show Walmart fashion in unexpected ways that make students ask, “That’s Walmart?”

Barefoot Wine "Sip Me I'm Irish" Campaign

Barefoot Case Study Video

Created in collaboration with my design partner, Susie Kastl.

Problem

Wine isn't considered as an option on St. Patrick's Day.

Insight

Wine drinkers switch to beer on St. Patrick’s Day because they don’t want to feel left out of the tradition.

Solution

Make wine feel like part of the tradition by making it on-theme.

Nebraska Goods Collective

New Brand Campaign

Problem

People like supporting small businesses, but often default to large retailers because they are easier and more visible.

Insight

People want to feel connected to what they’re buying.

Solution

Nebraska Goods Collective makes supporting local businesses as easy and accessible as everyday shopping.

Brand Activation

Nebraska Goods Collective hosts pop-ups at local markets and events showcasing small businesses for an in-person shopping experience. Each product features a small QR code that shoppers can scan to learn more. The code opens to a page on the NGC website where shoppers can learn the story behind the product — who made it and how it was made.
