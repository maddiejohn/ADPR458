“That’s Walmart?” Campaign

A campaign designed to shift Gen Z’s perception of Walmart fashion by making affordable style feel surprising, trendy, and worth sharing.

Case Study Video

Problem

Gen Z does not think of Walmart when they think about fashion.

Insight

College students love being surprised by style and value.

Solution

Show Walmart fashion in unexpected ways that make students ask, “That’s Walmart?”
