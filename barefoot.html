"Sip Me, I'm Irish" Campaign

A campaign concept created to position Barefoot Wine as a festive option for St. Patrick’s Day, a holiday usually dominated by beer.

Case Study Video

The Brief

The goal was to convince consumers to consider Barefoot Wine as a festive alternative to beer on St. Patrick’s Day. The challenge was that people automatically associate the holiday with beer and often do not consider wine as a festive party drink.

Problem

St. Patrick’s Day is seen as a beer holiday, so wine drinkers often switch to beer because they want to fit in with the tradition.

Insight

Wine drinkers switch to beer on St. Patrick’s Day because they do not want to feel left out of the celebration.

Solution

Make Barefoot feel like part of the St. Patrick’s Day tradition by turning wine into a festive, fun, and on-theme choice for the holiday.
